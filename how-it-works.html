How It Works

From the first ring to the
confirmed appointment.

Aligned Voice handles the full client journey automatically. Here's exactly what happens — step by step — when someone reaches out to your practice.

The Client Journey

What happens when someone calls

Most practices lose new clients between the inquiry and the booked appointment. Aligned Voice closes every one of those gaps.

📞

A client reaches out

It could be a call at 9pm, a text during your lunch break, or an inquiry through your website on a Sunday. Aligned Voice is available at all of them — and responds instantly, every time.

Inbound calls, missed calls, SMS inquiries, and web chat are all handled.
🤖

Aligned Voice has a real conversation

Not a phone tree. Not "press 1 for appointments." A natural, warm conversation that answers questions, collects information, and moves toward booking — in your practice's voice and tone.

Trained on your specific services, pricing, intake questions, and FAQs.
📅

Appointment booked directly into your calendar

If the client is ready to book, Aligned Voice checks live availability and confirms the appointment — no back-and-forth, no follow-up email required. Intake forms are collected at the same time.

Integrates directly with PracticeQ, Jane App, SimplePractice, and more.
🔄

If they're not ready, follow-up runs automatically

Not every inquiry converts on the first contact — and that's fine. Aligned Voice continues reaching out across voice, text, and email at smart intervals until the client responds or books.

Outbound follow-up is available on the Inbound + Outbound plan.
💚

Your team focuses on the work

By the time a client walks through your door, everything is handled. Intake collected. Appointment confirmed. Your front desk isn't fielding calls — they're present with the clients who are already there.

Aligned Voice hands off to your team whenever a situation needs a human touch.
See It In Action

A real conversation, start to finish

This is what an Aligned Voice call looks like for a new client inquiry at a physical therapy practice.

Live call · New client inquiry · 7:43 PM
Aligned Voice
Thanks for calling Best Life Physical Therapy. This is Maya — I'm here to help. Are you looking to schedule an appointment, or do you have a question about our services?
Caller
Hi, yeah — I've been having some lower back pain and I'm not sure if PT is the right thing for me.
Aligned Voice
That's actually one of the most common reasons people call us. Lower back pain responds really well to physical therapy — especially when it's been going on for more than a few weeks. Has this been ongoing, or is it more recent?
Caller
About two months now. My doctor mentioned PT but I keep putting it off.
Aligned Voice
Two months is a good time to get started — you haven't let it go on so long that it's harder to address. I can get you scheduled for an initial evaluation. We have openings this Thursday at 10am or Friday at 2pm. Does either of those work?
Caller
Thursday at 10 works.
Aligned Voice
Perfect. I'll book that for you now. Can I get your name and the best number to text a confirmation to?
✓ Appointment booked · Intake form sent via text · Calendar updated
Getting Set Up

Ready in days, not weeks

We handle the setup. You review and approve. Most practices are live within a week of signing on.

Step 1

We learn your practice

You fill out a simple onboarding form covering your services, pricing, scheduling preferences, and tone. We use this to configure Aligned Voice to sound like a natural extension of your team.

Step 2

We connect your systems

If you're on the booking integration plan, we connect directly to your practice management software. You review the setup and sign off before anything goes live.

Step 3

You forward your calls

No new hardware. No IT project. You forward your existing practice number to Aligned Voice — or publish the new number on your website. That's it. You're live.
