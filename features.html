Features

Everything your front desk
can't always be.

Aligned Voice handles the communication work that falls through the cracks — after hours, during sessions, and in every gap between your team's availability and your clients' timing.

Call Handling

Every call answered. Every time.

The most common reason wellness practices lose new clients is simple: no one picked up. Aligned Voice answers every inbound call — during your busiest hours, late at night, and on weekends — with a warm, natural conversation that sounds like your practice, not a call center.

  • 24/7 availability, no exceptions
  • Trained on your services, pricing, and FAQs
  • Natural conversation — not a phone tree
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Call recordings and transcripts for every interaction
  • Seamless handoff to your team when needed

of callers won't leave a voicemail — they'll call your competitor instead

Automatic Booking

From conversation to confirmed appointment.

Aligned Voice doesn't just collect information — it closes the loop. When a client is ready to schedule, it checks your live calendar and books directly into your practice management system. No follow-up email, no phone tag, no gaps.

  • Live calendar integration — real availability, always
  • Books directly into PracticeQ, Jane App, SimplePractice, and more
  • Collects intake information at the time of booking
  • Handles cancellations and rescheduling automatically
  • Waitlist management for high-demand appointment types
  • Confirmation texts and email reminders sent automatically
64%

appointment booking rate when clients can schedule in the same conversation

SMS & Text

Meet clients where they actually are.

Most people would rather text than call. Aligned Voice sends an instant text response to every missed call and handles full scheduling conversations via SMS — so clients who prefer texting can book without ever speaking to anyone.

  • Instant missed-call-to-text response
  • Full scheduling via SMS conversation
  • Automated follow-up sequences after first contact
  • Customized to your practice tone and voice
  • Two-way conversation — clients can reply naturally
  • HIPAA-compliant messaging throughout
98%

of text messages are read within 3 minutes of being received

Outbound Follow-Up

Inquiries that don't convert aren't lost — they're waiting.

Most practices give up on an inquiry after one or two attempts. Aligned Voice runs coordinated follow-up across voice, text, and email — at the right intervals, in the right order — until the client responds or books. This is where most of the revenue recovery happens.

  • Automated multi-channel follow-up sequences
  • Smart timing — not spammy, not forgettable
  • Stops automatically when client responds or books
  • Hands off to your team for complex situations
  • Re-engagement for cold leads from past months
  • Available on the Inbound + Outbound plan

Analytics

Know what's working and what's not.

Aligned Voice tracks every call, text, and booking — so you can see where clients come from, which inquiries converted, and where opportunities slipped away. No digging through call logs or spreadsheets.

  • Lead source attribution by channel
  • Inquiry-to-booking conversion rates
  • Call volume and peak hour reporting
  • Follow-up sequence performance
  • Revenue attribution per client acquisition channel
  • Monthly summary delivered to your inbox
