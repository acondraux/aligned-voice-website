Common questions

Do I need to replace my phone system?

No. Aligned Voice works alongside your existing setup. Most practices simply forward calls to the Aligned Voice number — no new hardware, no IT headaches.

How long does setup take?

Basic setup typically takes a few days. Booking integration depends on your practice management system — PracticeQ, Jane App, and SimplePractice are the fastest to connect.

What if clients realize they're talking to AI?

Aligned Voice is transparent when asked. Most clients don't ask — they just appreciate that someone answered. The experience is designed to feel warm and helpful, not robotic.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans, no long-term commitment required. If it's not working for your practice after the first month, you can cancel. That said, most practices see results within the first two weeks.

Can I customize how it sounds?

Yes. Aligned Voice is configured to your practice — your name, your tone, your specific services. It should feel like a natural extension of your team, not a generic answering service.

What practice management systems do you support?

PracticeQ, Jane App, SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, DrChrono, and more. If you don't see yours listed, ask — integrations are added regularly.
